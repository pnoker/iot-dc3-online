# Why a Physical AI Runtime

For a decade, IoT DC3 has been building the fundamentals: 28 protocol drivers, a cloud-native architecture, multi-tenancy and industrial-grade reliability.

But fundamentals are not positioning.

Today we define ourselves as a **Physical AI Runtime** — the layer that lets AI enter the physical world. A decade of industrial IoT engineering is our evidence, not our positioning.

The homepage says one thing: **Connect AI to the Physical World.** This page unfolds the worldview behind it.
